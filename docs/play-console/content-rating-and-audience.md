# Content rating (IARC) and target audience: draft answers

## Content rating questionnaire

- **Category:** "All other app types" (a utility / communication tool, not a game, social or reference app).
- Violence, fear, sexuality, language, controlled substances, crude humour, gambling: **No** for all. The app has no built-in content.
- **Does the app let users interact or exchange content with other people?** **Yes**: it makes phone calls (including video calls) and opens the messaging app. IARC treats calling as user interaction.
  - Is the interaction moderated? No (phone calls go through the carrier network).
- **Shares the user's location with others?** No.
- **Allows users to buy digital goods?** No.
- **Unrestricted internet access (web browser)?** No.
- **Is it a web browser or search engine?** No.

Expected result: **Everyone / PEGI 3 / IARC 3+** with an "Users Interact" descriptor. The rating is about content, so it's independent of the 18+ target audience below.

## Target audience and content

- **Target age groups:** **18 and over** only. (13–17 is possible, but call recording and the Families policy make under-18s more trouble than it's worth, and a dialer doesn't need to target children.)
- **Could the app unintentionally appeal to children?** No. The store listing and icon are plain and not aimed at children.
- **Families policy / Designed for Families:** don't opt in.

## Category and tags

- **App category:** Communication
- **Tags (pick up to 5):** Phone, Dialer, Call blocker, Caller ID, Contacts
- **Contact details:** email (required, shown publicly), website optional, phone optional.
