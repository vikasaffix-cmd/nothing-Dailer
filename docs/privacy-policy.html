NoDialer Privacy Policy

App: NoDialer (com.maihuvikas.dialer) Developer: maihuvikas Contact: [CONTACT EMAIL] Last updated: [DATE OF PUBLICATION]

NoDialer is a phone app: a keypad, call screen, call history, contacts and call blocking. It is made to keep your data on your phone. This policy explains what the app reads, what it stores, and what can leave your phone.

The short version

  • No ads. No analytics. No tracking. The app contains no advertising or analytics code, and we (the developer) receive no data from the app.
  • Your contacts, call history, recordings, notes and blocklist stay on your phone.
  • Data leaves your phone only if you turn on an optional online feature (online number lookup) or if Android's own backup copies the app's settings to your Google account.

What the app stores on your phone

The app stores these in its private storage: call recordings, call notes, call-back reminders, VIP and quiet-hours rules, your blocked numbers, block rules and spam reports, speed-dial keys, favourites order, caller names your network sent on earlier calls, voicemail "heard" marks, and your settings (language, font, vibration, lookup address). Uninstalling the app deletes all of it, except recordings you shared and contacts or call-log changes, which belong to Android.

Call recording law. Recording calls is your choice and your responsibility. In many places you must tell the other person, or get their permission, before you record. Recording is off until you turn it on. On most phones, only your side of the call is recorded unless you use the speaker.

What can leave your phone

1. Online number lookup (optional, off by default)

If you turn on Settings → Online number lookup and enter a lookup address, then when a call comes in from a number that isn't in your contacts, the app sends that phone number over HTTPS to the address you entered, to get a name or a spam warning. The app does not send your own number, your contacts or your call history.

That service is chosen and run by you or by a provider you picked. We don't run it and don't receive the data. Its own privacy policy applies. Turn the switch off to stop all lookups.

2. Backup

Android backup. If backup is on in your phone's settings, Android copies the app's settings and small data to your own Google account. That includes call notes, blocked numbers and rules, spam reports, caller names from your network, speed dial, SIM choices per contact, your settings including the lookup address and call pill, and a custom font if you added one. It also copies them to a new phone when you transfer. The app allows this cloud backup only when it is end-to-end encrypted with your screen lock. Call recordings and voicemail transcripts are never included. Google handles the backup and we can't see it. You can turn it off in Android Settings → Google → Backup.

Manual export. Settings → Backup & restore saves a backup file wherever you choose. Recordings and the lookup address are included only if you tick them. The file is yours: anyone you give it to can read it.

3. Things you choose to share

When you share a recording, call a number or send a message, the data goes to the app or person you chose. If WhatsApp is installed, NoDialer checks for it and shows which of your contacts use it, using information already in your phone's contacts. When you tap "Open chat", the number is passed to WhatsApp (wa.me), and WhatsApp's privacy policy applies from then on.

What we do NOT do

  • We don't collect, sell or share your personal data with anyone.
  • We don't use advertising IDs, analytics, crash reporting services or tracking.
  • We don't record calls unless you turn recording on.
  • We don't use Android accessibility services.

Children

NoDialer is not made for children under 13 and does not knowingly collect data from anyone.

Your choices

  • Turn off online lookup, voicemail transcription and call recording at any time in Settings.
  • Delete recordings, notes, blocked numbers and calls inside the app.
  • Revoke any permission in Android Settings → Apps → NoDialer → Permissions.
  • Uninstall the app to delete everything it stored.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the "Last updated" date. If the app starts sending new kinds of data, it will ask you first.
